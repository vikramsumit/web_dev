About Bioluminescence

Bioluminescence is the production and emission of light by living organisms. It's a natural phenomenon found in various creatures, from deep-sea fish to fireflies on land. This glowing ability serves purposes like attracting prey, deterring predators, or even communication.

In the oceans, creatures like jellyfish, squid, and plankton create stunning light displays. On land, fungi and insects add to the magic. This site celebrates these wonders, highlighting their beauty and scientific intrigue.
